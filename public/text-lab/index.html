Text lab

Pick a hero, a heading, and a body face.

63 candidates across three roles — 21 Hero Title, 21 Headings, 21 Body Text — grounded in Routstr's real mark: the rounded-square frame at /icon.svg, broken by a single bar rotated 112° through its center, the actual favicon shipped in app/layout.tsx. Every card sets the real Routstr wordmark (hero/headings: the word "Routstr" at each role's real usage size; body: an actual 2–3 sentence product paragraph) on both a light and a dark tile using Routstr's real background/foreground pairs (#fafafa/#0a0a0a and #111111/#e5e5e5), so each role is judged at the size it actually renders at on the site — not one undifferentiated font-pairing list. Scores are 1–10 from an adversarial critic pass; every card carries its concept, why it fits, and an honest weakness, including the low scorers. Two Hero candidates (marked below) have no verified free self-hosted source and are previewing on a system fallback stack rather than a fabricated font file.
